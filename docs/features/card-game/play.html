Play-by-play

How a snap works
  • 5 possessions each, every drive from your own 25. No punting: on 4th down, go for it or kick.
  • Stamina. The offense starts each drive with level 1 and bank 1. Cards cost stamina from the bank. After every snap the bank gains the current level (bank max 6). A play that isn't stuffed raises the level by 1 (max 5). The bank resets each drive.
  • Defense commits a read first (Run, Pass, or Blitz) face-down; then the offense commits a play card or attempts a field goal.
  • Correct read = held to the play's read yards (the smaller number on the card, often 0), and the offense's level holds. On a 2-point try a correct read stops the play. Blitz counts as a Pass read; a wrong Blitz against a Run gives up +3 yards.
  • Special cards are played in windows: Drive, Pre-snap, Post-reveal, Post-snap. Offense first, then defense, one card each per window. Counters can answer the card they name.
  • The defense banks 1 stamina per stop (max 5), starting each drive at 1.
  • Field goals: good from 40 yards or less; spend 2 stamina for +10 range. Kick distance = yards to the end zone + 17.
  • Ties go to overtime: rounds 1-2 are drives from the 25, then 2-point tries.
